04 CAPITAL

Capital

Connecting qualified clients with selected private-market opportunities.

I

Services

  • 01 Selected Investment Opportunities Access to vetted private-market opportunities within the network.
  • 02 Investor Introductions Connecting qualified investors with relevant situations.
  • 03 Capital Advisory Assessing capital structures and options within their context.
  • 04 Project Introductions Introducing capital-seeking projects to suitable investors.
  • 05 Strategic Capital Connections Building strategic connections between capital providers and seekers.
II

Typical starting points

SCENARIO 01

Access to vetted opportunities

A qualified investor seeking access to selected private-market opportunities is connected with relevant, vetted situations within the network.

SCENARIO 02

Raising capital for a project

A project sponsor seeking capital is connected with qualified investors through the network — subject to due diligence.

SCENARIO 03

Capital alongside structure and location

A mandate combines the search for capital with questions of structure or location. Byzaro connects it to Advisory and Real Estate where relevant.

III

How we work

01

Initial conversation

A confidential, non-binding conversation to understand your starting point and objectives.

02

Mandate & objective

Together we define the scope of the mandate and the concrete objectives.

03

Assembled from the network

We assemble the right team — from internal specialists and vetted partners.

04

Execution & support

We support the execution and remain your point of contact throughout the mandate.

IV

Working together

A division rarely stands alone.

Capital introductions are coordinated alongside Advisory structuring and Real Estate opportunities, assembled around each mandate.

Selected opportunities may only be available to eligible or qualified investors and are subject to jurisdiction, due diligence and applicable regulation. Byzaro is not a bank, investment fund or regulated financial institution.
